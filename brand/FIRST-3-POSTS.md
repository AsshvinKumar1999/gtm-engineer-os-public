# Launch plan: vertical, edge over Jordan, first 3 posts

Written 2026-11-08. Supersedes the three-lane menu in `PERSONAL-BRAND-PLAYBOOK.md` section 1 with one default pick.

## 1. The vertical: built environment and energy buildout

**Default pick:** companies that sell into construction, data center, solar, battery storage and grid projects (equipment, materials, staffing, insurance, financing, field software).

Why this one, using Jordan's own tests:
- **Compelled to publish.** Every project leaves dated public records before a vendor hears about it: building permits (city open-data portals), utility interconnection queues (ISO/RTO queue files), state contractor license boards, OSHA inspections, EPA and air permits, zoning and planning agendas, FERC filings. All free and all dated, so time-travel labels work.
- **The situation is visible.** "Filed a 40 MW interconnection request 9 months ago and just pulled site permits" is a pain-qualified segment you can prove with 2 to 5 records. No firmographic filter shows that.
- **The robot army is blind here.** Apollo and ZoomInfo have no permit or queue data, so the asymmetry is large.
- **Jordan never committed to it.** He used EIA and OSHA as one-off sources and moved on. Nobody owns this lane.
- **It animates well.** Permits and queue entries are natural particles moving on a map or a timeline.

**When to override it:** if your day job gives you 20 or more closed-won deals in another vertical, pick that one. Jordan's first rule beats any pick from me: start from the closed-won.
