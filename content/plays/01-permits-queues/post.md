## Caption (paste into LinkedIn)

Your next construction or energy deal is already public. Nobody is reading it.

Before a data center, solar farm or battery site buys anything, it leaves dated records:
→ a building permit
→ an interconnection queue entry
→ a zoning agenda item
→ a contractor license and an OSHA inspection for whoever is building it

Those records are free. Most sales tools do not carry them. So the seller who reads them first knows more about the project than the rep calling from a list.

The play in 5 steps:
1. Find who was forced to publish (city open data, ISO/RTO queues, license boards, OSHA, EPA)
2. Join the records into one dated project timeline
3. Score the situation, not the company: permit plus queue movement beats either alone
4. Write the message from the record, so it is useful even with no reply
5. Verify before anything ships: every quote matches its source, no AI tells, spend confirmed

Every step runs in code I keep in one repo.

If you sell into construction or energy: which public record would tell you a project is real?
